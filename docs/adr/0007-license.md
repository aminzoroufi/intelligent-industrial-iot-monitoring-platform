# ADR 0007: Portfolio source-available licensing

Status: accepted — 2026-08-11

The configured license mode is `PORTFOLIO_SOURCE_AVAILABLE`. Visitors may
inspect and evaluate the public portfolio, but receive no broad permission to
copy, modify, redistribute, sublicense, or use it commercially. The custom text
is explicitly not open source and is marked for legal review. Third-party works
retain their own licenses and notices.
